Keep the numbers honest.

A download click does not confirm a completed download or installation. Visits are page loads; reloading can create another visit. Browsers that block analytics, send a privacy signal, or disable JavaScript may not be counted.

Signed-in owner visits, automated browsers, and test-mode events are excluded. Reports cover the latest 90 days. Older records are pruned during analytics activity and dashboard refreshes. No analytics cookies, raw IP addresses, or cross-visit browser identifiers are stored.
