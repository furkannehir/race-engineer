Less personal data.

Pitward uses first-party website analytics to understand visits and clicks on the Windows installer, portable ZIP, and checksum links.

What our analytics records

  • The time of a visit or download click, and the referring hostname.
  • A broad screen-size group: mobile, tablet, or desktop.
  • A random page-load identifier that connects clicks to that page load. It is not saved in your browser or reused across visits.

Our analytics stores no tracking cookies, names, emails, raw IP addresses, full referring URLs, or persistent cross-visit browser identifiers. Reports cover the latest 90 days. Older records are pruned during analytics activity and dashboard refreshes. If the site is inactive, cleanup waits until the next such activity. Website hosting and security services may process technical requests under their own policies.

Your choice

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected. You can also exclude this browser below. Only this preference is saved locally; it is not a visitor identifier.

The application stays local

These analytics describe this website. They do not add telemetry to the Pitward desktop application or upload your racing data, driver preferences, conversations, or audio.
